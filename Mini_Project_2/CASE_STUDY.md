Case Study: End-to-End Coffee Sales ELT Pipeline & BI Analytics for BrewBeats
1. Executive Summary & Architecture OverviewTo modernize BrewBeats Coffee’s daily sales reporting, we designed and deployed an automated ELT (Extract, Load, Transform) data pipeline combining Snowflake, dbt (Data Build Tool), and Apache Airflow running inside a containerized Docker environment.
Storage & Compute (Snowflake): Acts as the centralized cloud data warehouse (BREWBEATS_DB.PUBLIC), storing raw point-of-sale coffee transaction records alongside transformed staging views and analytics-ready fact tables.
Transformation & Data Quality (dbt): Manages modular SQL transformations (dbt_snowflake_airflow), converting raw sales records into cleaned staging models (stg_coffee_sales) and daily revenue aggregations (fct_coffee_sales), backed by automated schema tests.
Orchestration & Scheduling (Apache Airflow): Runs locally via Docker Compose (coffee-sales-pipeline:1.0 with PostgreSQL metadata backend and LocalExecutor), scheduling and executing the end-to-end workflow daily via coffee_sales_dbt_pipeline.

2. Data Modeling & Transformation Layer (dbt)The transformation layer follows a two-tier modular architecture to separate raw data cleaning from business-level aggregation:
Staging Layer (models/staging/stg_coffee_sales.sql):Connects to the raw Snowflake source table via {{ source(...) }}.Standardizes column naming (sale_date, coffee_name, cash_type, card_id, amount), casts data types cleanly, and filters out malformed records.
Marts Layer (models/marts/fct_coffee_sales.sql):References {{ ref('stg_coffee_sales') }} to maintain explicit lineage.Aggregates transaction-level records to a daily grain (GROUP BY sale_date), computing key business metrics: total_orders, total_daily_revenue, and avg_order_value.Automated Data Quality Testing (models/marts/marts.yml & schema.yml):Configured 6 automated dbt data tests validating not_null constraints across critical fields (sale_date, coffee_name, amount, total_daily_revenue) and unique grain enforcement on fct_coffee_sales.sale_date.

3. Workflow Orchestration (Apache Airflow & Docker)To eliminate manual CLI execution and guarantee dependency order, the pipeline is orchestrated through coffee_sales_dbt_pipeline (dags/coffee-sales-dag.py) with schedule="@daily" and catchup=False:
Execution Order	-- Task ID -- Operator -- Purpose 
1 -- start_pipeline -- EmptyOperator -- Marks the logical entry point of the daily DAG run. 
2 -- verify_snowflake_connection -- BashOperator -- Runs dbt debug to validate Snowflake credentials, network connectivity, and profiles.yml pathing before transformation begins.
3 -- dbt_run_snowflake_models -- BashOperator -- Executes dbt run to materialize stg_coffee_sales and rebuild fct_coffee_sales in Snowflake.
4 -- dbt_test_snowflake_quality -- BashOperator -- Executes dbt test immediately after model builds to block silent data corruption.
5 -- end_pipeline -- EmptyOperator -- Confirms full end-to-end completion of all upstream tasks.


4. Key Engineering Challenges & ResolutionsBuilding and containerizing a cross-platform data pipeline on Windows surfaced several real-world infrastructure and schema issues that were systematically diagnosed and resolved:WSL 2 & Docker Resource Allocation: Running Airflow’s webserver, scheduler, and PostgreSQL metadata database concurrently on Windows initially caused container instability. Configuring .wslconfig (memory=4GB, processors=2) and correcting YAML service indentation in docker-compose.yaml stabilized container health checks and initialization (airflow-init exit code 0).Container Volume Mounting & Profile Pathing: Bridging Windows host directories (dbt_snowflake_airflow and %USERPROFILE%\.dbt) into the Linux container (/opt/airflow/dbt and /opt/airflow/.dbt) via DBT_PROFILES_DIR allowed Airflow’s BashOperator tasks to authenticate directly against Snowflake without hardcoding credentials in the DAG.Grain & Schema Alignment Between Models and Tests: During validation, dbt test flagged a grain mismatch (unique_fct_coffee_sales_sale_date) and column identifier mismatch (total_daily_revenue). Aligning the GROUP BY sale_date aggregation and output aliases in fct_coffee_sales.sql with marts.yml resolved both errors, resulting in 6/6 passing dbt tests and 5/5 dark-green Airflow tasks.

5. Key Business Insights & Recommendations
Analysis of the Power BI dashboard across all 11 reporting requirements revealed actionable operational patterns:

* **Core Revenue Performance:** BrewBeats generated **$115.43K** across **3.64K transactions**, maintaining a strong **$31.75 average transaction value**[cite: 8].
* **Menu Optimization (Volume vs. Revenue Leader):** **Americano with Milk** is the #1 best-selling coffee by transaction volume, whereas **Latte** generates the highest total revenue due to a higher unit price[cite: 8]. *Recommendation:* Feature Americano with Milk in morning combo promotions to drive repeat foot traffic, introduce upsell prompts toward higher-margin specialty drinks like Lattes, and prioritize inventory stock for milk and espresso beans.
* **Payment Method Split (Cash vs. Card):** Card payments dominate both transaction volume and total revenue (**~97%+**) compared to cash[cite: 8]. *Recommendation:* Prioritize contactless and card terminal reliability during peak rush hours and introduce digital loyalty rewards tied to card IDs.
* **Temporal Demand Patterns (Hour, Weekday & Monthly Trends):** Sales peak sharply around **10:00 AM** during the morning rush, with **Tuesday** and **Monday** driving the strongest weekday revenue, and **March** and **October** leading monthly revenue[cite: 9]. *Recommendation:* Align barista shift scheduling around the 10:00 AM rush window and run targeted mid-afternoon or late-week promotions to lift off-peak sales.
